Diversification

Many financial advisors say, quite reasonably, that a well-diversified portfolio across time and asset allocation is the single most reliable way for a beginner to manage risk while still capturing long-term growth.

The idea is to avoid putting all of your money into one stock, one sector, or even one asset class — spreading it out cushions the blow when any single holding falls.

For most new investors, a simple low-cost index fund is the easiest way to get broad diversification without picking individual securities.
