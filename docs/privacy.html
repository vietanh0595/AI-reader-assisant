Privacy Policy

BookSense AI · Effective 28 August 2026

This policy explains what BookSense AI (“the app”) does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past. If anything here is unclear, email booksenseai.support@gmail.com and ask.

The short version.

Your notes, highlights and conversations stay on your phone. We never see them.

If you switch on Whole-Book AI for a book, that book’s text is uploaded to our server so it can be searched. Nothing else about your reading is uploaded.

Your questions and the AI’s answers are sent to OpenAI to be answered, and are not stored on our server afterwards.

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.

Who is responsible

BookSense AI is an independent app built and operated by a single developer. For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, that developer is the data controller. Contact: booksenseai.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Most of what you create in the app never leaves your device. It is stored in the app’s own storage and is removed when you delete the app.

  • The books you import, and their full text
  • Your highlights, saved notes and quick-action results
  • Your Ask-the-book conversations
  • Your reading position and progress
  • Your app settings

These are not backed up to us. If you lose your phone, we cannot recover them. They may be included in your own iCloud or iTunes device backup, which is under Apple’s control and not ours.

What is sent to our server

When you sign in

Signing in is handled by Auth0, using Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive and store: your email address, your display name if the provider supplies one, and an identifier that lets us recognise you next time. We never receive or store your password. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only ever see the relay address Apple gives us.

When you switch on Whole-Book AI for a book

This is the one feature that uploads real content, and it only runs on books you explicitly enable it for.

What we storeWhy
The full text of that book, split into paragraphsSo questions can be answered from anywhere in the book, not just the page you are on
A mathematical representation of each passage (an “embedding”)So the app can find the passages relevant to your question
The book’s title, author, file name and formatTo show it in your library and tell your books apart
Mind maps generated for that bookSo you can leave and come back without regenerating them

Books you have not enabled Whole-Book AI for are never uploaded.

Copyright. You are responsible for having the right to use the files you import. We store book text solely to provide this feature to you, we do not make it available to anyone else, and we do not use it to build anything.

When you use an AI feature

Each request sends the passage you selected, or the relevant excerpts from the book, along with your question. We do not store the question or the answer. They pass through our server, are answered, and are gone.

Rate limiting

Your IP address is used, in memory only, to stop any one device making an unreasonable number of AI requests. It is not written to a database and does not persist beyond a short rolling window.

Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

ProviderWhat they receiveWhy
OpenAI Your question and the book excerpts needed to answer it Generates the answer. Sent through OpenAI’s API, which under their terms is not used to train their models. See OpenAI’s policies.
Auth0 (Okta) Your sign-in details Handles authentication so we never hold a password.
Render and Supabase Everything described under “What is sent to our server” Host the server and the database.
Sentry Crash reports: what went wrong, your device model, OS version, and app version So crashes get found and fixed. Crash reports do not include your books, notes or questions.
Apple TestFlight and App Store data, under Apple’s own policies Distributes the app.

Camera scans stay on your phone

When you scan a printed page, the text is recognised on your device using Apple’s built-in Vision framework. The photograph is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. You can confirm this yourself: turn on Airplane Mode and scan a page. It still works.

The recognised text then behaves like any other book in the app — it stays on your device unless you enable Whole-Book AI for it.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data. Not to anyone, for any price.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not use your books, notes or questions to train AI models — ours or anyone else’s.
  • We do not read your notes or conversations. They are on your phone; we have no copy.

How long we keep things

  • Questions and answers — not stored at all.
  • Uploaded book text and its index — until you delete that book from your library, or delete your account.
  • Your account record — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Crash reports — kept by Sentry under their retention schedule, typically 90 days.

Deleting your data

A single book’s uploaded text: delete that book from your library. Removing a book deletes it from your phone and deletes its uploaded text, its search index and its mind maps from our server at the same time.

Everything on your phone: delete the app. Your notes, highlights, conversations and imported books go with it.

Your whole account: email booksenseai.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We will delete your account record and every book you have uploaded, and confirm when it is done. We aim to do this within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Email booksenseai.support@gmail.com and we will action it.

Our legal basis for processing is the performance of our contract with you — that is, actually providing the app you asked for — and our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and free from abuse.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

BookSense AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Where your data is held

Our server and database are hosted in data centres operated by Render and Supabase. Our providers, including OpenAI, may process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EU, it is protected by the standard contractual clauses those providers operate under.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted in transit. Your sign-in is handled by Auth0 and we never hold your password. Book text is stored so that it is only reachable by your own account.

No service can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect. This page always shows the current version.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: booksenseai.support@gmail.com. A real person reads it.
